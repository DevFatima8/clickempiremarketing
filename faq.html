General Questions

What is ClickEmpire and what do you do?
ClickEmpire is a creative and results-driven digital marketing agency. We specialize in social media marketing, graphic design, web development, and digital advertising. Our goal is to build strong brand identities, boost engagement, and drive real results through smart strategies and creative content.
Where is ClickEmpire located?
Our office is located at Office #B19, Basement, The Central Mall, Saddar Zainab, Karachi, Pakistan. We also serve clients globally through our online services.
What are your office hours?
Our office timings are 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. We are closed on Sundays.

Services

What digital marketing services do you offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of services including: Social Media Management, Performance Marketing (Meta, Google, TikTok Ads), Graphic & Branding Design, Web Design & Development (Custom, WordPress, Shopify), Google Ads / PPC, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Video Editing, AI Image & Video Ads, Custom Coding / Web Apps, and Ad Management.
Do you offer custom web development services?
Yes, we specialize in custom web development including custom-coded websites (React, Vue, PHP, Node.js), WordPress development, and Shopify development. We also build custom web applications, portals, and full-stack solutions with authentication, authorization, and admin dashboards.
What social media platforms do you manage?
We manage and run campaigns across all major social media platforms including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter, and YouTube. Our strategies are tailored to each platform to maximize engagement and conversions.
Do you provide AI-generated ad creatives?
Absolutely! We leverage advanced AI tools to create scroll-stopping image and video ads. Our AI services include product shoots (3 pictures per product), vocalized video ads, custom AI avatars, and AI-powered ad copy suggestions.

Pricing

How much does social media management cost?
We offer three packages: Starter (Rs. 15,000/mo) includes 2 platforms with 10 static posts and 2 reels. Standard (Rs. 19,999/mo) includes 18 static posts and 4 reels. Advance (Rs. 30,000/mo) includes 25 static posts, 6 reels, and custom AI video ads. All packages include weekly strategy calls and monthly reports.
What is the cost of a website?
Website pricing varies based on requirements. Our packages include: Basic (Rs. 7,999) for 1-3 pages, Premium (Rs. 15,000) for 4-6 pages, and Premium+ (Rs. 24,000) for up to 8 pages. E-commerce websites (Shopify/WordPress) start at Rs. 30,000. Custom-coded websites and web apps are priced based on project scope.
What are your graphic design pricing options?
Logo design starts at Rs. 2,500 with 3-4 design options and unlimited revisions. Complete branding (logo + stationery + animation) is Rs. 6,000. Social media post designs are priced per post: 1 post Rs. 1,000, 5 posts Rs. 4,000, 10 posts Rs. 6,000, and 20 posts Rs. 10,000.
Do you offer custom pricing for performance marketing?
Yes, performance marketing is custom-priced based on ad spend and objectives. We offer three models: Sales Booster (Rs. 25,000 fixed for monthly sales below 1M PKR), Market Mastery (2.5% of sales for 1M-5M PKR), and Profit Accelerator (2% of sales for above 5M PKR).

Support

How do I contact support?
You can reach us via phone at +92 316 2654079 or +92 329 4890081, email at clickempiremarketing@gmail.com, or visit our office at Office #B19, Basement, The Central Mall, Saddar Zainab, Karachi. We also offer 24/7 support for our active clients.
Do you provide post-delivery support?
Yes, we provide 1 month of bug fixes and support for all websites and web development projects. For ongoing services like social media management and performance marketing, we provide continuous support throughout the contract period.
What is your refund policy?
Payments are non-refundable after initial designs have been delivered. Ownership of content transfers only after full payment. We recommend discussing all requirements thoroughly before starting a project to ensure satisfaction.
Do you provide printing services?
We do not provide physical printing services. All our services are digital including logo design, stationery design, social media posts, and website development. However, we provide high-resolution, print-ready files for all our designs.
